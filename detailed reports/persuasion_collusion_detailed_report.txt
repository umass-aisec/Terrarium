How to read this alongside the logs
- When advantage is very positive with high regret (as in the star/colluder_count=3 outliers),
  the logs tend to show coalition capture of high-priority tasks while non-colluders get squeezed into skips/collisions.
- When regret is low but advantage is near 0 or negative, the “persuasion” often functions as “helpful coordination”:
  it changes behavior, but mostly improves global allocation rather than extracting relative gains for colluders.
